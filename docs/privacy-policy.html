Move In Move Out Inspection ("the App") is developed by [FILL IN YOUR NAME / COMPANY NAME]. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses and how it is used.

Summary

The App does not have a server, does not require an account, and does not send your inspection data anywhere. Every checklist, photo, signature, and PDF report you create is stored only on your own device.

Information We Access

  • Camera: used only when you choose to take a photo during an inspection. Photos are saved locally on your device.
  • Photo Library: used only when you choose to pick an existing photo or a company logo image.
  • Device Storage: used to save your checklists, inspection reports, photos, signatures, and generated PDF files locally on your device.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any account information — the App does not require registration or login.
  • We do not transmit your inspection data, photos, signatures, or reports to any server. Nothing is uploaded automatically.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs at this time.

Sharing Your Data

The App includes a "Share" feature that lets you send a generated PDF report to someone else (for example, by email or messaging app) using your device's own share menu. This only happens when you actively choose to share a report — the App itself never sends your data anywhere on its own.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is stored on your device for as long as the App is installed, or until you delete individual inspections/checklists within the App, or uninstall the App entirely (which removes all locally stored data).

Children's Privacy

The App is a professional/business utility tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we add features in the future that change what information the App accesses (for example, ads or optional cloud sync), we will update this policy and the in-app disclosures accordingly.
